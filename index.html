SELECTED PROJECT PROPOSAL

Smart IoT-Based Heavy Metal Detection and Intelligent Diversion System

An IoT-based industrial wastewater safety concept that combines multi-parameter water-quality sensors with AI-assisted verification to identify possible heavy-metal contamination and automatically divert contaminated wastewater for treatment.

Water Sensors → ESP32 Controller → Random Forest AI → Contamination Check → Intelligent Diversion

The proposed system monitors heavy metals and supporting water-quality parameters. When contamination exceeds configured safe limits, it can trigger alerts and divert wastewater to a recovery tank for treatment, followed by a final water-quality check.
